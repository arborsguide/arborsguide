    Understanding color, more truthfully, is about understanding the world around you.

    Arbor's Guide is a website/book that officially started in January of 2025. Arbor's Guide is intended to be a centralized, practical, and comprehensive source of information and tools to help myself and others understand the world around them, with a special focus on topics related to color. For now its mostly just a place for me to list alt-codes and formulas I tend to forget, but eventually I'll get around to writing some guides.
