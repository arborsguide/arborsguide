    • The Gradient Issue and Emission

    Classic portrayals of the electromagnetic spectrum (EMS) are often wrong. The most egregious error thats perpetuated is showing a rainbow spanning the EMS from radio to gamma rays. Most representations are at least partially accurate by limiting their rainbow gradient to the actual visible region of the spectrum.
    However, it is quite easy when producing a figure like this to apply a simple rainbow gradient, wherein each signature color (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet) is equally spaced according to their rgb values. In reality, if you were to observe a relatively monochromatic light-source which scans through wavelengths 1nm at a time, you would find that some regions of color are much wider, and some much narrower.
